About This Policy

Trix Score - Partners (the “App”) is a scorekeeping utility for the physical partnership Trix card game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App lets you enter team names and game scores and maintains round results, match history, and saved matches.

This information is stored locally on your device to enable the App to work and preserve your game progress.

Data Collection and Sharing

The App does not require an account and does not transmit your team names, game scores, or match history to the developer's servers.

The App does not contain developer-operated analytics, advertising, or tracking features and does not sell your information.

Managing Your Information

You can manage matches and scores using the features provided in the App.

The information remains on your device unless you choose to remove it using the App's available controls or remove the App and its locally stored data through iOS.

Device-level backups and other Apple services, if enabled by you, are governed by Apple's settings and privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the App's functionality or data practices change. The current version will be available at this URL, with the effective date updated as appropriate.
