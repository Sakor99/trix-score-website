About Trix Score

Trix Score is an easy-to-use scorekeeping app for the Trix Partners card game. Track scores, review completed rounds, and focus on enjoying the game with friends and family.

Features

  • Automatic score calculations
  • Live scoreboard for two teams
  • Four-round match tracking
  • Correct previous scores and undo entries
  • Save and review matches on your device
